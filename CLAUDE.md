# Influencer personas

Five AI personas for short-form channels. Reference IDs, Soul IDs and voice IDs live in memory
(`influencer-personas-project.md`); copy them here when they stabilise.

## Rules
- A character's face must be IDENTICAL across every generation. Use trained Soul IDs, never reference images.
- **If a character's face is on screen, they are talking (lip-synced). Voice-over only over faceless b-roll.** Decided 2026-10-02 after reviewing `open_the door.mp4`.
- No real event names, logos or readable signage in any prompt.
- No alcohol in hands. No "party". No sheer/see-through/bare wording. These trip the Higgsfield filter.

## Elizabeth long-form series
- 10 episodes, each 10-15 min, built from ~2 min sections (one scene per section), 10-15 s clips.
- Three talking setups: CU, bench two-shot, over-the-shoulder. Rotate every clip.
- Caption card with the question on the first clip of every section.
- Spine (life order): 1 handkerchief 1943 / 2 provided for / 3 the bank / 4 her own name / 5 three suitcases / 6 the developer / 7 the loss / 8 the board / 9 the piano player / 10 nobody is coming.
- Canon: born 1943 (83 in 2026), Lancashire mill town, grew up on a street with bomb gaps (NOT bombed herself, Blitz was 1941). Mother on mill wage, four jars on the mantel: rent, coal, tin, sweets. Typist in a Bootle shipping office at 17. Bootle two-up two-down at 19 in 1962, 450 pounds, father co-signed, nurse tenant upstairs. Married at 22 in 1965; 40 pounds in a handkerchief from mother that morning; lent it to husband for a deposit, never returned. Marriage sold Bootle house; secret account (opened 1975, 11 pounds) bought it back ~1980 (Ep 5). Left March 1981 with three suitcases. Charleston townhouse lost 1987 (Ep 7 candidate). Father: allotment behind the gasworks, gave her a row at 8, "late, but not too late"; sold early potatoes for her dance dress at 14; shook her hand on the platform at 17, "ring before, not after"; rent never late, handshake with the rent man. 1983 she rang a landlord before being late, got 14 days. Street canon (longform-the-street-i-grew-up-on): rag-and-bone man secretly owned a house with a garden; Mrs Edna Hartley never repaid, mother's pencil tally inside the pantry door; library card at 7, father left school at 12 and could barely read; short-time winter 1957, mending, canal reeds, firelighters, "live on half" ever since; mother short-measured at the market cloth stall, stood silent at the counter until he paid up; first wage at 15 handed over unopened, "stand like you're not waiting for Friday".
