# Elizabeth. Episode 5: Three Suitcases

Target runtime 12:00 to 12:30. Six sections of about 2 min, each one scene, cut on scene change.
Clip length 10 to 15 s (Seedance 2.5). 62 clips.

## Shot types
- **CU**: close-up, Elizabeth talking to camera, lip-synced (the "What's the biggest mistake" shot)
- **2S**: wide two-shot on the garden bench, Elizabeth and interviewer, lip-synced
- **OTS**: over the interviewer's shoulder onto Elizabeth, lip-synced
- **BR**: faceless b-roll, voice-over only. No face of any person, ever. Hands, objects, rooms, backs, silhouettes.
- **CARD**: question caption card over the first clip of each section (same style as the Short)

Rule: if Elizabeth's face is on screen, she is talking. No exceptions.
Rotate CU / 2S / OTS every clip in talking sections so no setup holds longer than 15 s.

Period for b-roll: England, 1975 to 1981. Warm faded Kodak look, grain, no modern objects.
