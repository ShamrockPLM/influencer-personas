# Elizabeth. Episode 1: The Handkerchief

Target runtime 12:00 to 12:30. Six sections of about 2 min, each one scene, cut on scene change.
Clip length 10 to 15 s (Seedance 2.5). 62 clips.

Shot types and rules: see episode-05. CU / 2S / OTS are talking and lip-synced. BR is faceless b-roll with voice-over. CARD is the question caption on the first clip of each section.

Period for b-roll: Lancashire mill town 1950 to 1958, Bootle 1962, a Lancashire chapel 1965. Cold grey northern light, soot on brick, faded Kodachrome, grain, no modern objects.

Canon locked in this episode: born 1943, mill town, mother on a mill wage, father at the works. Four jars on the mantel: rent, coal, tin, sweets. Bootle two-up two-down bought at 19 in 1962 with father co-signing, a nurse renting the upstairs. Married at 22 in 1965. Forty pounds in a handkerchief on the wedding morning.
